What remains

We build because memory is fragile.

A cave wall, a temple, a dome, a sculpture, a tomb, a school, a house over water.

They do not share one material, religion, country, or century. What they share is a human refusal to let experience vanish.

Each room leaves a different kind of trace: image, order, light, wonder, body, grief, method, landscape.

Architecture is never only shelter. Art is never only object. Both are ways of asking time to remember.
